# Something Fishy: Story Bible

Storyline for the road trips (Quiet Lake → River bend → Old quarry → Sea pier). This document covers
writing and design only; the build lives in GAME_DESIGN.md slices. Written 2026-11-24.

**Decided with the user (2026-11-24):** whimsical-spooky tone, an original creature (never named
Cthulhu), a gentle "it remembers her" ending, the bottle notes written by a previous angler "W.",
Pajkel as the skeptic who sleeps through everything, interest nods at wink level, chapters per place
that wait for her, and no story locked behind the night kit. **Personal notes from Pajkel to Alex are
cut for now** and parked for a far-future polish pass (see §6, ❓1).

---

## 1. Premise and tone

All the water in this game is connected underneath: the lake, the river, the flooded quarry and the
sea. Something very old and very large sleeps at the bottom of it all: **the Sleeper**, the
"something fishy" of the title. It isn't hungry or angry. It's lonely, and it is polite in an odd,
ancient way: everyone who meets it forgets, so nobody gets scared. Its dreams leak out as strange
fish, and the Pale One is the first of them. Years ago an angler who signs as **W.** noticed, and
started leaving notes in bottles so they wouldn't forget. Alex follows W.'s notes from place to
place. Pajkel misses every spooky moment because he's asleep in the car, and Bestia senses all of
them. At the Sea pier, at dawn, the Sleeper does something it has never done before: it remembers
someone. It remembers her.

**Tone**
- **Cozy first.** Nothing chases her, nothing is lost, nothing punishes her. Every shiver is followed
  by a warm or funny beat, usually the next morning.
- **Whimsical-spooky, not horror.** It's odd and wondrous rather than threatening. The scariest beat is "it blinked."
- **Three shivers in the whole story** (Q4 the extra star, O5 the eye, S5 the dawn). Everything else is
  lore, warmth or jokes.
- **Running gags carry the warmth:** Pajkel sleeps through every spooky event, Bestia keeps every
  stick, and the worm-thief duck follows them on the road trip.
- **Wink-level nods** to her interests: dinosaurs (the quarry's living fossil), Trivium (the finale
  is called "In Waves", and no lyrics are quoted), anime (a "final boss" line), and Lovecraft
  (the Sleeper, a "W." signature, a town called Dunwater).

## 3. Chapters

**Rules**
- **At most one chapter per real date**, gated like the bait box (real local date, clock-change guard).
- Each place has its own chapter list. **She has to be at that place** to get its chapters, and
  chapters wait for her: missing days skips nothing and never piles up.
- "Day N" means the Nth chapter day *at that place*, not the calendar.
- **Trigger** chapters (a checklist finished, arriving somewhere) also take that day's slot. If the
  slot was already used, they come the next day.
- **No chapter needs the night kit.** A night chapter shows its night version if she is there
  between 21:00 and 05:00, and the **day version** (listed under it) if she plays without the kit or
  only by day. Each chapter is delivered once, in one version.
- **Pajkel's lines** follow the bedtime rule: he only speaks when awake and on the dock. A line due
  while he sleeps waits until 05:00, which is the running gag.
- **Delivery:** each chapter appears within ~1–2 real minutes of the visit's start, so it's never
  missed. Bottle chapters use a **sealed story bottle** (wax seal) that doesn't drift away, so it
  looks different from the random bottles.
- Limits: bottle ≤ 90 characters, bubble ≤ 40 characters (plain ASCII), card 2–4 lines of
  ≤ ~38 characters each, log fact one sentence.

## 4. New lake moments per place

Same rules as slice 14 (one at a time, no fights, ~3 min gap). **Story-only** moments (Q4, Q5, R1,
O3, O4, O5, S1, S5) play once as chapters; the moments below are the repeatable pool.

**Quiet Lake (new)**
- **The duck's sandwich run** (06–19, rare after Q2): the duck circles the dock, eyeing the planks. Tap: it pretends it wasn't doing anything and paddles off.
- **The stick comes back** (01–05, uncanny): one of Bestia's old sticks drifts back to shore by itself. Tap: it sinks. Float: "...no take?"
- **Fog over the deep spot** (03–05): a small round patch of fog hangs over the far water, and nowhere else. Tap: it thins away.
- **Winking star** (after the finale, 21–04): the extra star in the lake winks. Tap: a sparkle and 5–15 coins, like a shooting star.

**River bend**
- **Kingfisher** (06–18): a blue flash dives and comes up with a tiny fish. Tap: it flies off chirping.
- **Otter** (dawn/dusk): an otter floats on its back, eating. Tap: it rolls and dives, then pops up again.
- **Upstream lantern** (22–04, uncanny): a small lantern light drifts upstream with no boat under it. Tap: it goes out softly.
- **Paper boat** (any time): a folded paper boat floats past; ~1 in 4 holds 2–5 worms. Tap: catch it.
- **Heron** (dawn): a grey heron stands perfectly still in the shallows and stares at Bestia. Bestia stares back. Tap: it stalks off, offended.

**Old quarry**
- **Falling pebble** (any time): a pebble drops from the cliff and plinks into the water, and the rings travel very far in the clear water. No tap.
- **Fossil glint** (day, rare): a glint in the cliff wall. Tap: a small "fossil found" card and a few coins (could become a fossil collection later; see §7).
- **Bat swarm** (dusk): bats pour out of a crack in the cliff. Tap: one loops around the lantern or the dock.
- **Warm steam** (01–05, uncanny): thin steam rises from one spot as if the water were warm. Tap: it stops.
- **Deep glow** (night, uncanny, rare): a faint pale glow far down, very slow. Tap: it dims, and the crickets come back.

**Sea pier**
- **Gull heist** (day): a gull lands on the rail and stares at your bait tin. Tap: it shrieks and flies off.
- **Seal** (day): a seal's head pops up, looks at Bestia, and Bestia loses her mind (boof boof). Tap: it sinks out of sight.
- **Lighthouse skip** (night): the lighthouse beam sweeps, and once it pauses on the water just a beat too long. No tap.
- **Glowing wave** (21–03): a wave lights up blue-green as it breaks (bioluminescence). Tap: a sparkle trail.
- **A polite tentacle** (03–05, uncanny, rare): a tentacle tip rises out of the sea far off and waves, slowly. Tap: Alex waves back, and it sinks away.

## 5. Checklists (bundles) per place

Item lines are short and in the game's voice. Coin items mean paying in, and fish items mean handing
in a fish instead of selling it. Exact contents get tuned against size stars and quests later.

**Quiet Lake: "The Doorstep"**
- **Dawn Chorus**: roach, rudd, bleak caught at dawn. *"Early fish for early birds."*
- **Night Shift**: eel, zander, catfish. *"Things that bite after bedtime."*
- **The Bottom of Things**: bream, tench, crucian carp, carp. *"Ask the ones who live down there."*
- **Shiny Ones**: any 3 gold-star fish. *"Show the lake you're serious."*
- **Duck Tax**: 1 bleak. *"For the duck. You know why."*
- **Petrol Money**: 500 coins. *"The DS4 won't drive on vibes."*

**River bend: "Against the Current"**
- **Swift Water**: trout, grayling, chub. *"Fish that never sit still."*
- **The Hunter**: asp (and perch or pike). *"It hits the surface like a slap."*
- **Ferry Fare**: 800 coins. *"W. always charged the fare. Even to fish."*
- **Upstream Mail**: 3 fish caught at dusk. *"The Ferryman works the evening shift."*
- **A Stick for the Road**: any 1 fish while Bestia is playing fetch. *"She has her priorities."* (optional fun item; see ❓6)

**Old quarry: "Deep Time"**
- **Clear Water**: 3 quarry species caught in daylight. *"If you can see them, they can see you."*
- **Living Fossils**: 2 of the quarry's odd catches. *"Older than they look. Much older."*
- **Warm Spring**: 2 fish caught 01:00–05:00, or at dusk without the kit. *"The water came up warm."*
- **Gate Key Fund**: 1500 coins. *"Someone has to fix the gate properly."*

**Sea pier: "In Waves"**
- **Tide Table**: 3 sea species caught at different times of day. *"The sea keeps its own clock."*
- **Gull Tax**: 2 herring. *"Pay them, or they take the chips."*
- **Lighthouse Oil**: 2500 coins. *"Keep the light on. Someone's watching it."*
- **Every Bottle Ends Here**: one gold-star fish from each place. *"Bring a little of everywhere."*

## 6. Open questions

❓1. **Personal notes from Pajkel**: cut for now. Where should they go later?
➡️ Far-future polish pass: about 6 sealed bottles signed "— P." at milestones (the first chapter,
each arrival, the finale), plus a "Notes" page in the log to reread them. The chapter list leaves
room: one could ride along with Q1, R1, O1, S1, S5 and E1 without changing their text.

❓2. **How does a chapter announce itself?** If she plays for 2 minutes and misses it, the story stalls.
➡️ A sealed story bottle that waits at the dock (it never drifts off), and a small wax-seal badge on
the notice board until today's chapter is seen. Moment chapters replay at the start of the next
visit if she closes the game mid-moment.

❓3. **Should the duck really follow them to the river** (and to the sea)?
➡️ Yes, it's the warmest running gag. From R4 on, the same duck sprite appears at every place, and
the sea's gulls make it look noble by comparison.

❓4. **The Pale Scale pendant needs a wardrobe.** Nothing like that exists yet.
➡️ A tiny "Wardrobe" card from the ⚙ panel with 2–3 items: the band tee (default), the dino hoodie
(already planned as a cosmetic), and the Pale Scale. The pendant is a 2–3 pixel pale sparkle on
her chest. If a wardrobe is too much, the pendant is simply always on after S5.

❓5. **Does the Pale One stay at Quiet Lake only?**
➡️ Yes. Home keeps its legend, and the epilogue sends her back home. Other places get their own legendaries.

❓6. **Checklist items tied to companions** ("a fish while Bestia plays fetch"): cute, but are they too fiddly?
➡️ Keep at most one per place, and only one that happens anyway during normal play (fetch runs when
she's idle). Cut it if it confuses the play-test.

❓7. **What about the extra fact lines** (O3 adds a second line to the Pale One, R5 adds one to the chub)?
The log shows one fact per species.
➡️ Use the "rotating facts" idea from GAME_DESIGN Ideas: story facts join a species' fact pool once
unlocked, and the log shows the newest. The E1 fact replaces the Pale One's fact for good.

❓8. **Post-story bottle notes**: what keeps the random pool fresh after the finale?
➡️ 4–6 new random notes, unlocked by S5, mostly from W. in their warm new home ("Weather's lovely.
The fish here are purple. — W."), plus one that is signed "— The Lake" again: "Thank you for
visiting."

❓9. **Real-world dates and places**: "1931" and the town name "Dunwater".
➡️ Keep both: 1931 is a quiet nod to *The Shadow over Innsmouth* (written that year), and Dunwater
is invented. Swap Dunwater for a Polish-sounding name if the Polish translation comes.

❓10. **Fish rosters for the new places** (this is the build session's call, flagged here so the story fits):
➡️ River: trout, grayling, chub, asp, barbel, nase, plus the Ferryman. Quarry: whitefish, vendace,
crayfish (a "catch"), a pale blind cave fish ("odd"), a "glass perch" you can see through ("odd"),
plus the Ironjaw. Sea: herring, cod, flounder, garfish, mackerel, plus the Other Angler.

## 7. Future places (after the Sea pier)

Each extends the Sleeper's world in a new direction, and none needs a scary beat.

- **The Warm Place** (a lagoon): W.'s new home, where W. finally appears in person (a cheerful old
  angler in a straw hat; we never see their face up close). Purple fish, turtles, and **dinosaur
  bones in the cliffs** (a "lost world" lagoon). Legendary: *the Coelacanth*, a real living fossil
  ("Declared extinct in 1938, it never read the paper"). Ties off W.'s story.
- **Sunken village** (a reservoir): a dam flooded an old village, and on still nights a church bell
  rings under the water. Cozy-creepy at its best: lanterns in drowned windows, a fish that lives in
  the bell tower. Legendary: *the Bellringer*, a huge old carp.
- **Frozen pond** (ice fishing, winter): drill a hole and fish through the ice. The Sleeper's breath
  rises as a warm patch that never freezes. Cozy: thermos, a snowman Pajkel builds, Bestia eating
  snow. Legendary: *the Frostjaw* pike. Good for a seasonal event.
- **Underground lake** (a cave under the quarry): reached via the quarry's "doorstep" ring of stones.
  Blind white fish, glowing mushrooms, Bestia's echo boof. This is where the Pale One comes from.
  Legendary: *the Firstborn*, the Pale One's much older sibling (post-story, very hard).
- **Mountain tarn** (a high lake above the clouds): very quiet and very cold, with the clearest
  stars. The extra star is here too: the Sleeper keeps an eye on her everywhere, kindly.
  Legendary: *the Cloud Char*.
- **The Lighthouse island** (an extension of the Sea pier): the keeper's island and the Sleeper's
  front door. A post-story place where the Sleeper's small dreams wash up as collectable odd fish.
  The most "Lovecraft" place, played cozy.
- **Night market / festival** (an event, not a place): once a season, a lantern festival at Quiet
  Lake. NPC anglers' tournament (GAME_DESIGN Ideas), food stalls, and the duck in a tiny hat.
