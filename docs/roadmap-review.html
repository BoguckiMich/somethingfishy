Open decisions

Each overturns or settles something in GAME_DESIGN.md or RIVER_BEND.md. The line under each is my recommendation.

Perfect catch grace. Allow ~300 ms outside the zone before a run stops being perfect?

Yes. Aim for a 15–25% perfect rate for a skilled player on max gear.

Gear gates. Tie the Carbon rod to “Bottom of Things” and Fluoro/Pro to “Night Shift” instead of a bundle count?

Yes. It keeps the gate meaning “you've fished deep”.

Notice board. Remove the top-right button and keep only the shore sign?

Yes. If testers still miss the sign, add a badge in the bottom bar, not top-right.

Naps. Keep them free, but trim deep-night prices and add a short cooldown?

Yes, a 2-minute cooldown at most. Never an energy bar.

River current. Drop the forced auto reel-in until aimed casting exists, and give river fights a pattern rather than one-sided drift?

Yes. Widen the Ferry Pike band to 18–26 m too.

Render scale. Cap it at 3× (from 4×) for battery and heat?

Yes, then check text sharpness on your phone; it cuts the pixels drawn per frame by about 44% on 1080p phones.

Bag into Shop. Fold the Bag button into “Shop · n/10” so Settings gets a full 44 px button?

Your call. It saves taps but changes muscle memory.

Pajkel's tap lines. Allow subtle inside jokes, not only universal cozy lines?

Universal in the public repo; inside jokes could go in the hidden “— P.” bottles instead.

Polish before River Bend. Ship the Quiet Lake polish first, and bundle zone notches and river sound into M2?

Yes. Alex's next session gets warmer; nothing is touched twice.

Bedtime dim mode. A Settings switch that warms and dims the whole screen?

Only if Alex plays in a dark room a lot; it is easy to add later.

Personal notes. Start the hidden “— P.” bottles on real-day milestones (day 3, 7, 14, 30)?

Your call. You said “later” before; it is the strongest gift moment in the idea pool.

Idea pool

Everything proposed so far and not scheduled, from GAME_DESIGN.md (yours and the testers'), the external review, and its retention ideas. The source is in blue.

GAME_DESIGN · userAimed casting + lake features

Slide the thumb to aim; reeds, lilies, a sunken log, a drop-off each hold different fish. Build both together.

GAME_DESIGN · Stardew-likeTreasure in the catch bar

A chest drifts through the bar; keep it in the zone to grab coins, bait or a cosmetic. Missing it costs nothing.

GAME_DESIGN · testerSwitch between owned rods

Fish shallow with a light rod for fun or quests; needs rods to differ beyond “bigger is better”.

GAME_DESIGN · userSpecies that need a special bait

Glow bait at night, fish strip for a predator; a “needs …” hint in the log.

GAME_DESIGN · testerAchievements + total weight per species

Badges and a running weight per fish in the log.

GAME_DESIGN · userLivelier fight reactions

Pajkel leans in and cheers during the fight, Bestia watches the line.

GAME_DESIGN · userRotating fish facts

A pool of facts per species, maybe one on every catch; unlockable facts tied to chapters (R5, O3, E1).

GAME_DESIGN · userPersonal bottle notes from Pajkel

Hidden notes for Alex, with a Notes page to reread them. You said “far future”.

GAME_DESIGN · userNPC tournaments, later online leaderboards

“Biggest bream before 18:00” against named NPC anglers; online needs a backend and accounts.

GAME_DESIGN · v2aWorm farm

Soil plots you feed; worms multiply over game days, feed decides the worm type.

GAME_DESIGN · v2bFields and bait crafting

Grow corn and wheat; craft hook bait and groundbait from recipes.

GAME_DESIGN · v2cSpinning

Lures and wobblers; retrieve speed and depth decide what attacks.

GAME_DESIGN · v2dFeeder fishing

Heavy cast, bite alarm, maybe two rods; for carp, bream, catfish.

ROADMAP · after M5Trophy wall or aquarium on the dock

Show off the favourite catches where Alex fishes.

ROADMAP · after M5Seasonal events

Frozen pond, lantern festival; more places from STORY §7; cloud sync.
